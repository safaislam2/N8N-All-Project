# 🚀 AI Lead Generation & Market Research Agent

An AI-powered lead generation and business research system built with **n8n, AI, Google Maps/SerpApi, and automation**.

This system collects business requirements from a user and automatically researches relevant businesses, competitors, reviews, ratings, and market information to generate a clean and useful business research report.

## ✨ Features

- 🔎 Find relevant businesses and potential leads
- 📍 Search businesses based on location
- 🏢 Research business information
- ⭐ Analyze ratings and reviews
- 🏆 Identify competitors
- 📊 Find market opportunities and weaknesses
- 🤖 Generate an AI-powered research report
- 📄 Organize important information clearly
- 📧 Automatically deliver the final report
